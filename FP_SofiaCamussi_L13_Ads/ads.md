# FP | Sofia Camussi | Facebook Ads

Five Facebook and Instagram ads for Pareto Talent's lead magnet, **Get Your Business Out of Your Head in 10 Minutes: The Founder Manual Builder**. One ad per persona (P1 to P5). Each ad has a different persona, pain, angle and funnel stage. This is copy and image work only. No ad is run or funded.

**Audience for all five:** US founders and owner-operators of service, agency, coaching and expert businesses.
**Landing continuity:** every ad promises exactly what the page delivers. P1 to P4: a free, 10-minute, one-page Founder Manual (landing page, form anchor "Build my Founder Manual"). P5: the 20-minute Matching Call, with the manual as the thing you bring (booking page).
**Landing URLs:** `[SITE_URL]` is a placeholder, to be filled with the published site address. Each ad link carries `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=founder-manual&utm_content=p1` (p2, p3, p4, p5 for the others).

## Image prompts

### Master style prompt (reusable brand recipe)

Paste this first in every image request, then add the scene prompt below it.

> 3D clay-style miniature diorama, soft felt and clay textures, Pixar-like warm human character with a kind, capable expression, standing on a round stepped stone platform. Deep teal and forest green set with wood furniture, a woven rug, monstera leaves and small succulents. Single warm top spotlight from above, cinematic dark background #050A0E, surfaces #0B1119 and #0F1923. Neon emerald light trails and soft glowing particles in #10B981, #34D399 and #6EE7B7, with one small amber accent #E0973F. Clean, calm, "calm after chaos" mood. Square 1:1 composition, subject in the lower two thirds, empty dark space across the top for a headline. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks anywhere in the image.

### Scene prompts (add under the master prompt)

**Scene 1, Ad 1, P1 Memory Runner** (point: everything lives in one head, then it resolves)
> A tired but hopeful founder in beige knitwear sits at a wooden desk at center. Loose papers, sticky notes and tiny glowing envelopes orbit tightly around their head in a messy ring, some falling to the rug. To the right of the head, the same pieces stream along an emerald light trail and fold into one single clean glowing page that floats above an open laptop. A small clock on the desk shows a calm, relaxed time. The mess is on the left, the order is on the right.

**Scene 2, Ad 2, P2 Once-Burned** (point: the seats were empty because nobody had a manual)
> Three wooden desks in a row on a stepped stone platform. The first two desks are empty with pushed-back chairs and a faint dust of light. The founder stands at the side, arms crossed, looking at them thoughtfully. The third desk glows: an open one-page manual lies in the middle, emerald light rising from it, and a warm clay assistant character sits there, reading it with a smile and a coffee. Chairs, lamps and plants match across all three desks.

**Scene 3, Ad 3, P3 No-Time-To-Train** (point: weeks of training collapse into ten minutes)
> On the left, a tall teetering stack of thick training binders and folders reaches nearly out of frame, fading into shadow. On the right, a busy founder in beige knitwear holds a single glowing page and hands it across a desk to a new clay character who takes it with both hands. Between the two sides, a large hourglass with only a small amount of sand left, its top glowing emerald. Emerald light trails connect the page to the new hire's laptop. Succulents and a monstera frame the desk.

**Scene 4, Ad 4, P4 Stage-Bound Expert** (point: your voice and judgment travel through one page)
> A clay speaker in a smart jacket stands at a small lectern on a round stage platform under a bright top spotlight, mid-gesture to an audience of a few small empty chairs. From the speaker's chest, a ribbon of emerald light shaped like a sound wave flows along the floor and up into a single glowing page held by a calm assistant character seated at a laptop at the side of the stage. The laptop screen glows green. Monstera leaves and succulents frame the scene.

**Scene 5, Ad 5, P5 Ready Buyer** (point: the manual in hand, three matches ready to meet)

Three different clay characters in distinct outfits (a cardigan, a blazer, a scarf) walk toward the founder along three separate neon emerald light trails, each one arriving at a small round pedestal. Above the founder, a small floating calendar page glows with a single highlighted day. The mood is decisive, warm and unhurried. Plants and soft rug texture ground the set.

On the right, a clay assistant at a laptop. A long emerald light trail runs across the whole width connecting them, with small succulents and a monstera at each end. ### How the imagery was produced

The images were generated with Canva's AI image generator through the Canva connector, one generation per scene (six in total, no regenerations), the full-resolution files were exported from Canva, and the overlay text and layout were done in HTML and rendered to PNG with headless Chrome. Method: the master prompt was built from Pareto's existing dioramas (hero-diorama, offer-core, offer-mastermind, founder-delegator, paretotalent.com/img/), deconstructed into one reusable recipe covering style, palette hex codes, lighting and composition. One scene prompt was then written per persona, each showing the point of that ad. All text (overlays, headlines) was added afterwards in a layout tool, so no words are rendered by the image model. Same palette, same character style and same top-light across all five ads, the landing page hero and the email header.
