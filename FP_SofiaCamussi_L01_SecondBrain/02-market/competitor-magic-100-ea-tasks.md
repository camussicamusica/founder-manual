| Weakness | Nine inputs including a required phone for a list PDF; 100 generic tasks with no prioritisation or cost; no personalisation or proof; nothing on time zone, language or country; an unexplained checkbox; name, email and phone passed in a URL is a privacy smell. |
| How Pareto beats it | The same task idea sorted by hours saved a week and tagged by what a Latin America-based Right Hand working the founder's hours can own on day 1 versus day 30; 3 to 4 fields instead of 9; instant on-page download plus calendar. |
