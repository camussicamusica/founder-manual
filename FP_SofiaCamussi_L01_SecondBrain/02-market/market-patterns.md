# Market patterns and the gap

Twelve live lead magnets were found in the VA, EA and offshore hiring space; three were chosen for format variety: [[competitor-athena-delegation-assessment]], [[competitor-magic-100-ea-tasks]], [[competitor-belay-delegation-worksheet]].

## Patterns
1. Everybody leads with "what can I hand off?". Task lists and frameworks dominate: BELAY "automate, delegate, delete, and keep", Magic "100 tasks", Wing Assistant "50 jobs", Time etc "task ideas". None offers a number a founder can compare to what they pay today.
2. Capture is thin, qualification is rare. Most gates are 1 to 3 fields. Only Magic qualifies hard (company, title, size, phone). Nobody asks what a placement agency needs: stage, hours a week to hand off, which tasks, time-zone needs.
3. The post-opt-in step is almost always the same: thank-you, file, then "Book a call" or "Find your EA". Athena's thank-you is a second sales page; Magic goes to a calendar; BELAY swaps the form for "Schedule a Call". Nobody delivers a personalised next step.
4. Many gates are soft. BELAY and Magic expose the PDF URL in page code; Oceans gives templates ungated; Somewhere's calculator is ungated.
5. Tools beat PDFs on engagement but are rare: Athena (quiz), Somewhere (calculator), Time etc (wizard). Athena writes the score into the CRM.
6. Job seekers contaminate lists. Athena blocks 8 countries in code, Time etc asks "assistant or looking for work", Magic checks for non-business emails. A Latin America-based firm needs an intent question, not an IP filter.
7. Nobody owns the Latin America story. Somewhere compares LatAm to the Philippines and recommends the Philippines for EAs; BELAY says "U.S.-based"; Athena says "in your time zone".

## The gap
Every competitor answers "what can I hand off?". Nobody answers "how do I hand it off without weeks of training?". Pareto already owns the hand-off-what angle with the Delegation Audit and the HIRE book ([[existing-lead-magnets]]). A third magnet that repeats it would add nothing. The decision built on this gap is in [[lead-magnet-brief]]; the founders it targets are in [[personas]] (especially P1 and P3).
