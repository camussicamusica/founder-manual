# Day 10: what Ivan asked for in the final project

Speaker: Ivan Bunin. The project is a lead magnet for Pareto Talent, due end of day October 7th. The same brief is printed in the deck, see [[final-project-guidelines]]. Excerpts are short and verbatim.

## The ask in one paragraph
Build a lead magnet that qualifies founders and leads to a call about the Right Hand Program. Nine parts, delivered as one PDF. Goals per the deck: position Pareto as the authority on delegation and founder freedom, grow the list, move qualified founders to a call. Asked in the Q&A whether the landing page speaks to entrepreneurs or to new talent, Ivan answered "Entrepreneurs." (L4745).

## The nine parts (as spoken)
1. Second brain and market research. L2073: "You'll need to feed it at least 3 resources." L2077: "research three real lead magnets from competitors in the similar space." L2085: "what is their format promise, who it is for, and how they capture and qualify, and what happens right after someone opts in." L2089-2093: define the ICP and "at least, three founder personas by cause, in the client words." This brain and [[personas]] answer Part 1.
2. Business map of the full journey. L2109: "Link every step to a real asset behind it." L2113: "anything that you couldn't build, mark it as a gap ... if it's red, it's red. If it's green, it's green."
3. ClickUp breakdown plus one SOP. L2137: "every task should get an owner, a deadline, a status, and priority." L2149-2153: "Write one SOP from this project so that next lead magnet launch. We'll follow that exact process."
4. Design the lead magnet and offer. L2165-2169: "a resource that somebody wants, and they want it so much that they give you their email address." Formats at L2173: PDF, checklist, quiz, calculator, mini app, Claude skill, agent. L2177: "that lead magnet needs to qualify them." L2181: use the MAGIC formula and the value equation. L2185: define who counts as qualified. L2193: write the next-step offer. Our answer: [[lead-magnet-brief]].
5. Build it and take it live. L2213: it must be published, shareable, "is it gated?". L2217: delivered by email the moment someone opts in.
6. Funnel: landing page, qualifying form, qualified and not-qualified pages. L2257: qualified and not qualified are sent to different pages.
7. CRM and workflows. L2265-2289: pipeline (opt-in, qualified, call booked, client, plus an unqualified stage); opt-in workflow; 3 emails for qualified; 1 email for unqualified; booking confirmation and reminder. L2289-2297: "All of the workflows, by default, should be turned off."
8. Five Facebook ads with AI imagery. L2313: "build 5 genuinely different messages." L2317: "You do not need to run the ads, you just need to create a copy and the image." L2329: "the same visual identity across the ads, pages, emails, lead magnets."
9. Two-minute Loom. L2341-2345: explain how the lead magnet moves a founder toward a call about the Right Hand Program and walk through all assets.

## How it is handed in (L2349-2405)
One PDF. Section 1: name, application email, lead magnet title, final tool. Section 2: link index, each link on its own line with an exact label; if not built, "write the label, and just put a dash. Do not put any fake links" (L2373). File name FP_FirstnameLastname_ParetoBootcamp. A submission page takes the email used at registration and a link to the PDF. See [[final-project-guidelines]] for labels and naming.

## Extra mile (L2409-2425)
"What else would you bring to the table to make this lead magnet a success?" Extras count inside the scoring category they belong to.

## Automatic disqualifiers (L2429-2457)
- A link that does not work. L2437: "you click Command Shift N, open incognito window and test it."
- A login wall: L2445: "If there is a login vault ... it doesn't work."
- Shortened links (Bitly and similar): "disqualifier."
- Image-only PDF: "I cannot click anywhere, it doesn't work."
- Missing or wrong labels.
- Submission after the deadline: L2457: "If you submit after the deadline, sorry."

## Rubric (L2461-2541)
Five categories, five points each, twenty-five total: 1 strategy and offer (research, ICP, personas, offer); 2 lead magnet and funnel (live, working, landing page); 3 CRM and automation (pipelines, workflows, right emails in order); 4 creative (ads by funnel stage, on-brand imagery); 5 project management and presentation (structure, SOPs, naming, PDF structure, Loom).
- 20 or more: pass, certificate, interview with the CSM team, then onboarding (L2517-2525).
- 15 to 19: certificate, no interview (L2529-2533).
- 0 to 14: no certificate (L2537-2541).
- L3837-3857: the 20 can be distributed any way ("5, 5 ... 4 4 ... 2"). L3365: "No one will be able to do 100%." L3385: prioritize.
- L4413 on PDF length: "It depends on how concise and structured your communication is."
