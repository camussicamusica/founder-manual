# Matching page and Matching Call

## /matching (https://paretotalent.com/matching)
- Headline: "Reclaim 40+ hours a month with a pre-trained Right Hand." Labels: "For 7 & 8-figure founders", "founders doing $100K+/mo".
- Body: "a top‑1% Right Hand who owns your inbox, calendar, follow-ups and ops ... Fully fluent in the modern AI stack, and trained before they ever touch your business. No recruiting. No training. No management headache."
- Price line: "Month-to-month, from $3,600/mo." See [[pareto-talent-identity]].
- Flow: watch the short video, answer the survey, book the Matching Call, within 24 hours get 3 pre-vetted profiles, interview, pick one, start month-to-month.
- The page says "Two quick questions below"; the embedded survey actually asks about eight items: full name, email, phone, primary business website, "What's ONE HOUR of your time worth to you?" (Under $50 / $50-$100 / $100-$300 / $300+), "What would a perfect Executive Assistant be for you?" (free text), whether the person is the one who will work with the Right Hand, and "How did you hear about us?". On submit it routes to /matching-call.
- Entry URLs: https://paretotalent.com/matching (main), https://paretotalent.com/matching?ref=delegation-audit (from the quiz in [[existing-lead-magnets]]), https://paretotalent.com/referrals (referral intros go to the Matching Call).

## /matching-call (https://paretotalent.com/matching-call)
- Heading: "Step 2 of 2 · Almost there ... You're in. Lock in your Matching Call."
- Embedded calendar (GoHighLevel widget): https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/booking/6zqcYsw9cth5fvvIU9rA. Fallback: hello@ParetoTalent.com.
- "What happens on the call": 1 Map what to hand off, 2 See your fit ("whether it's a people problem or a delegation problem"), 3 Get 3 matches in 24 hours.
- Footer: "By booking, you agree to receive reminders ... by email and text".
- Call length is not stated ("a quick call"). Kasim reviews matches personally per the site.

## Names of the call across pages
"Matching Call" (site), "Pareto Matching Call" (HIRE funnel), "Discovery Call" (Done-For-You Hiring page, https://paretotalent.com/done-for-you-hiring).

## Matching policy (https://paretotalent.com/matching-policy)
The matching process is "a non-negotiable boundary" and "the core matching system itself is not customizable". Do not promise founders custom filters.

The Matching Call is the destination for qualified founders in [[lead-magnet-brief]]. The booking page pattern (step 2 of 2, what happens on the call) is mirrored in the Day 10 brief: see [[day-10-final-project-brief]].
