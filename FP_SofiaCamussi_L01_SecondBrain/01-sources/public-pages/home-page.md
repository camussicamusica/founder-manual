## Right Hand versus a VA (verbatim pairs, "Why a VA Alone Will Never Solve This")
| VA, "Reactive" | Right Hand, "Proactive" |
|---|---|
| Waits: "You asked me to send the email, so I sent it." | Anticipates: "I noticed the client hasn't responded in 7 days — want me to follow up? I've already drafted the message." |
| Excuses: "You didn't tell me to check for that." | Owns it: "I saw the invoice hadn't been paid. I've sent a reminder and flagged it for you." |
| Stops: "The task is done. What's next?" | Closes loops: "I've updated the tracker, blocked your time, and flagged a conflict on Thursday." |
| Limits: "I only do what's in my job description." | Thinks ahead: "I've been thinking about that problem you mentioned last week. Here's what I found." |
| Can't: "I don't know how to use that tool." | Builds: "I built a Claude agent for that workflow. I'll show you how it works Friday." |
