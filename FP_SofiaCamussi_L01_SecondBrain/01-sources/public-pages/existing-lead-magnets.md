# Pareto's existing lead magnets

Pareto already runs two lead magnets (plus smaller free assets). A third one must be different. See [[lead-magnet-brief]] and [[market-patterns]]. Nothing here was submitted; form fields were read from page markup and public code.

## 1. HIRE book funnel: https://thehirebook.com
- Format: free book (PDF) plus resources, GoHighLevel funnel. Ads run on Facebook (D10 line 2633). Built by Gaspar (D10 line 2641).
- Promise (hero): "Learn how to FIND & MANAGE the team that will make you MILLIONS." CTA: "GET THE BOOK FOR FREE". Popup: "GET HIRE + THE PARETO TALENT FRAMEWORK. Full PDF Copy + ALL Resources FOR FREE! Download the complete toolkit Kasim used to build his multi-million dollar agency remote team! Fill out this form to claim it!"
- Page sections: testimonials, "Find. Hire. Delegate.", "WHO SHOULD READ HIRE?", Amazon links, author bio, and a giveaway ("We'll Fund Your EA for an Entire Year", through a free Skool community).
- Opt-in form (3 slides). Contact: Full Name*, Email*, Phone*, Referrer, "How did you hear about us?". Then:
  - "How many people work in your company?"* Only me, 2-5, 6-10, 10-25, 25-50, 50+
  - "What's your business' annual revenue?"* Under $100k, $100k to $500k, $500k to $1M, $1M to $5M, $5M to $10M, $10M+
  - "Are you planning to hire in the next 6 months?"* Yes, No, Maybe
  - "Are you handling low-impact / operational tasks? (Such as emails, calendar, administrative work, etc.)" Yes or No
  - "Which of the following best describes your current situation? (Select all that apply)"*: evenings and weekends; strategic work delayed by operational tasks; rescheduling personal commitments; dissatisfied with work-life balance
  - "How much time did you spend on your last hiring process from start to finish?"* Less than 10 hours, 10-20, 20-40, 40+, Haven't hired before
  - "What's your monthly budget for a high-performing remote employee?"* Under $3,000, $3,000-$4,000, $4,000-$5,000, $5,000+
- After opt-in (from the funnel's page list in public code, plus Ivan on the recording): qualified goes to "Book a Call (Qualified) - Pareto Matching Call" (/book-a-call); unqualified goes to "Thank you (Unqualified) - Book Only" (/thank-you-hire-2). Ivan, D10 lines 2661-2665: "your book is on the way ... here's our video for the VSL. We would like to jump on a quick call with you." The exact thank-you copy and the email sequence were NOT observed (UNVERIFIED).

## 2. Delegation Audit: https://paretotalent.com/delegation-audit
- Format: AI-assisted quiz app, built by Gaspar (D10 lines 2669-2689). Live.
- Hero: "Free 3-minute diagnostic. What's your Delegation Score? Your founder type, the exact role to offload first, a 90-day plan to hand it off, and the real dollar cost of staying in the weeds." Button: "Get my score →". Microcopy: "11 questions · no email required · instant result". Social line: "Trusted by 100+ founders · 93% retention at 12 months" (93% is on the do-not-use list in [[proof-bank]]).
- The page code shows 11 questions but the progress counter reads "01/ 10"; which is current is UNVERIFIED.
- No email gate before the result. Questions:
  1. How big is your team right now? (Just me / 2-5 / 6-15 / 16+)
  2. Which of these sounds most like you right now? (8 archetypes, including "Everything still routes through me", "It's faster if I just do it myself", "I've hired help before", "Clients pay for time with me", "I've been through a few assistants already")
  3. Real talk: how many hours a week do you actually work? (Under 40 to 70+)
  4. Which of these are you still doing yourself? (10 tasks: inbox, scheduling, follow-ups, invoicing, data entry, social, support, travel, research, hiring)
  5. When did you last take a full week off, truly unplugged?
  6. What is an hour of your best work worth? ($50 to $1,000+)
  7. How many hours a week vanish into admin and repetitive work? (Under 5 / 5-10 / 10-20 / 20+)
  8. If you vanished for two weeks, your business would... (Keep growing / Run just fine / Start to wobble / Fall apart)
  9. How do you feel at the end of a typical day? (Energized / Fine / Drained / Completely fried)
  10. Have you tried delegating before?
  11. What's really holding you back from getting help? (The cost / I don't know what to delegate / Tried before, got burned / No time to train someone)
- Output: a score with bands, a founder type (for example The Player-Coach, The Once-Burned Founder, The Stage-Bound Founder), a ranked delegation list, a 90-day plan and a dollar cost card; it also mentions the Freedom 40 guarantee.
- After the result: the main CTA "See who'd fit your business →" goes to https://paretotalent.com/matching?ref=delegation-audit. A secondary newsletter form (First Name, Last Name, Email): "We send one delegation breakdown a week. Real hand-offs, not theory."
- Ivan on the recording: the quiz gives "You're becoming the bottleneck. Here is analysis ... ready to book a call with us" (D10 lines 2685-2689).

## Why this matters
Both existing magnets answer "what can I hand off and what does it cost me?". Neither helps a founder hand work off without weeks of training. That is the gap used in [[lead-magnet-brief]]. Competitors and their patterns: [[market-patterns]].
