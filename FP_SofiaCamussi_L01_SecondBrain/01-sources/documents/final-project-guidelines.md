# Final project guidelines (summary)

Source: the Day 10 slide deck. The deck points to a fuller guidelines folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pXrKsuFGnnSAvwJvJTKxtZoJ2UkcD02u?usp=sharing . That folder was NOT available to this build, so the full label list below is incomplete and marked. What Ivan said about the same material is in [[day-10-final-project-brief]].

## Goal (slide 34)
Launch a lead magnet for qualified founders. Build the funnel: a page that sells it, a form that qualifies, two different next steps. Qualified founders book a call with Pareto; everyone else gets the resource and a nurture sequence. Goals: position Pareto as the authority on delegation and founder freedom; grow the list; move qualified founders to a call about the Right Hand Program. Due end of day, October 7th.

## The nine parts (slide 35)
1 Second Brain and market research · 2 Business Map of the funnel journey · 3 ClickUp breakdown and SOP · 4 Design the lead magnet and offer · 5 Build it and take it live · 6 Build the funnel (landing page, qualifying form, booking page, thank-you page) · 7 CRM and automation workflows · 8 Five Facebook ads and AI imagery · 9 Two-minute Loom.

## Part 1 requirements (slide 36), the part this brain answers
- Build a Second Brain, as a Claude Project or in another LLM, trained on Pareto Talent.
- Feed it at least three source types: paretotalent.com, the Wall of Love, the Day 1 story and Pareto's bootcamp frameworks; at least one transcript, one document and one public page.
- Research 3 real lead magnets from competitors in the VA or hiring space: format, promise, who it is for, how they capture and qualify, what happens right after someone opts in.
- Define Pareto's ICP and at least 3 founder personas by cause, in client words.
Answers: [[README]], [[02-market/_MOC]], [[icp]], [[personas]].

## Lead magnet rules (slides 38 and 39)
Format: PDF guide, checklist, quiz, calculator, mini app (Ivan adds Claude skill or agent). Solves one specific problem for a founder in under 10 minutes. Named with the MAGIC formula and the Value Equation. Define who counts as qualified (exact form criteria) and write the next-step offer. Must be useful even if the founder never talks to Pareto, on-brand (palette, fonts, tone), published and shareable, delivered automatically by email on opt-in. Our answer: [[lead-magnet-brief]].

## Funnel and CRM requirements (slides 40 and 41)
- Landing page sections: Hero, What's inside, Who it's for, Proof with real Pareto numbers, CTA with the form.
- Qualifying form: contact details plus the qualification questions. Qualified is redirected to a booking page with an embedded calendar; not qualified goes to a thank-you page that confirms and delivers the resource.
- Pipeline: opted in, qualified, call booked, client, plus an unqualified stage.
- Workflows: opt-in (deliver, tag qualified or unqualified, create opportunity, notify yourself); qualified follow-up (3 emails); unqualified nurture (1 email delivering the magnet); booking workflow (confirmation and reminder). Test the full chain. Ivan: workflows are left OFF by default (D10 lines 2289-2297).

## Creative and presentation (slide 42)
Five Facebook ads, image plus copy, five genuinely different messages built from persona and angle work, each with hook, offer, qualifier, proof, mechanism, call to action. One visual identity across ads, pages, emails and the lead magnet. Two-minute Loom walking through lead magnet, funnel, workflows and ads.

## How it is handed in (slide 43)
One PDF, three sections: Cover (full name, application email, lead magnet title, funnel tool used); Link Index (every link on its own line with its exact label; if not built, write the label followed by a dash); Walkthrough (one heading per part: what you built, why, its link label). Submit at bootcamp.paretotalent.com/finalproject. Reminders on the slide: one single PDF; deadline end of day October 7th; "Don't share your project publicly."

## Naming rules
- PDF file name: FP_FirstnameLastname_ParetoBootcamp.pdf (slide 43; D10 line 2373).
- Bootcamp naming habits for tools: give forms and workflows clear names, "not Form 9, Form 7"; tag format like `form::<name>::step one::IB` and `calendar::02-booked-IB`; put work in a folder with your own name (Day 6 lines 2585, 2657-2673, 2749; see [[bootcamp-frameworks]]).
- Project convention used by this student for this build (our decision, not a Pareto rule): tool names "FP | Sofia Camussi | Item Name"; files and folders `FP_SofiaCamussi_L##_ItemName`. See [[2026-10-03-build-log]].

## Automatic disqualifiers (slide 45)
A link that does not open (errors, private, asks for access; one broken link disqualifies the whole project); login walls (set everything to "anyone with the link"); shortened links (write every URL in full, starting with https://); an image-only PDF (text must be selectable); missing or renamed labels; late submission (after end of day, October 7th, no extensions). Ivan's test: open every link in an incognito window (D10 line 2437).

Note for the builder: slide 43 says "Don't share your project publicly" while slide 45 needs every link to open without login. The safe reading is "anyone with the link", not a publicly listed or promoted folder.
