# Pareto Talent: identity and offer

## Who they are
Pareto Talent places full-time, Latin America-based, English-fluent, AI-trained Right Hands (executive assistants who act as operators) with founders. Co-founders: Kasim Aslam and Ivan Bunin. The origin story is in [[day-01-pareto-story]]. Public stats strip and proof status are in [[proof-bank]].

## The offer in one sentence
The Right Hand Program: a full-time, Latin America-based, English-fluent, AI-trained Right Hand matched to a founder by Predictive Index (PI) behavioral profile, bundled with a weekly Delegation Mastermind, a Client Success Manager, a Second Brain OS and three guarantees, from $3,600 a month. (Status: VERIFIED-SITE for each part; the one-sentence wording is our synthesis. Source: [[home-page]].)

## How matching works (home page, "How We Match You")
1. PI Behavioral Assessment: personality profiling to ensure compatibility.
2. Role Expectation Alignment: map the founder's needs, work style and preferences.
3. Compatibility Interview: meet the top 3 candidates, interview all three, choose one "or walk away owing nothing".
4. 30-Day Integration: structured onboarding with a dedicated Success Manager.

Founder path (from /matching): answer the survey, book the Matching Call, get 3 pre-vetted profiles within 24 hours, interview, pick one, start month-to-month. Details in [[matching-and-call]]. The matching process is "a non-negotiable boundary" and "not customizable" (/matching-policy), so never promise custom filters.

## The seven components (home page)
1. The Core: the AI-powered Right Hand (full-time, LatAm-based, fluent English, top 1% of cohort, the founder's hours).
2. The Delegation Mastermind: weekly live calls with Kasim and 100 founders ("7 Laws of Delegation").
3. Pareto Right Hand Community: peer network of 250+ operators; core AI tool subscriptions are covered by Pareto.
4. 10-Day AI Bootcamp plus continuous training: 40+ hours before placement, then 104+ sessions a year.
5. Three Guarantees.
6. Pareto Second Brain OS: decisions, SOPs, vendors, meeting notes in one system.
7. Client Success Manager.

## Guarantees, with their conditions (home page wording)
1. Freedom 40: "Attend the Delegation Mastermind, follow the onboarding plan, and meet with your Right Hand 10 minutes daily. If you don't get at least 40 hours back within your first 30 days, your next month is on us." It is conditional. Never drop the three conditions.
2. Matching: "If we don't find a match you're truly excited about, you owe nothing. No contracts until you find your person. We present 3+ hand-picked candidates, you interview them and choose."
3. Lifetime Replacement: "If the match is ever not right, for any reason, at any time, we replace your Right Hand at no cost. No fees. No waiting period. Forever."

## Right Hand versus assistant
Pareto's own framing: a VA waits and stops at the task; a Right Hand anticipates, owns the outcome and closes loops. Verbatim pairs are in [[home-page]]; the spoken version from Ivan is in [[day-01-pareto-story]]. Rule: say "Right Hand" or "executive assistant", never "virtual assistant" (Ivan, D1 line 1425).

## Who it is for, and not for
- Pain profile: the founder who is the bottleneck, drowning in inbox and calendar, often burned by a VA before. See [[icp]] and [[personas]].
- Revenue band on Pareto's own pages is inconsistent: "$100K+/mo" on /matching, "$50K-$1M/month" on the Freedom 40 page and webinar. Which is current is UNVERIFIED. Our working ICP uses the Day 8 figure; see [[icp]].
- Not for: founders who want to customize the matching filters.
