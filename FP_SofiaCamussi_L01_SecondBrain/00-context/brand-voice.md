# Brand voice

## Tone
Direct, confident, founder-to-founder, slightly blunt. Diagnosis first ("not a people problem, a delegation problem"), then the cost of waiting. Short declarative sentences, second person ("you", "your Right Hand"), concrete verbs (owns, closes loops, builds). Heavy on risk reversal ("owe nothing", "no scenario where you lose"). In spoken form (Ivan) it is radically honest and treats the Right Hand as a partner, not a task-taker. Source: [[home-page]], [[day-01-pareto-story]].

## Words to use
bottleneck, delegation, Right Hand, executive assistant, operator, top 1%, hand-picked, matched, trained, AI-powered, founder, "your time zone", "owe nothing", "close the loop", "proactive".

## Words and moves to avoid
- "virtual assistant" or "VA" for Pareto's product (Ivan, D1 line 1425; D1 line 1005: "we are not a VA or BPO company").
- Button copy "Submit". Use an action phrase (D6 L1809-1813, see [[bootcamp-frameworks]]).
- A hook that is only "Do you need an executive assistant?" (D8 L1765-1773).
- Any number not marked verified in [[proof-bank]].
- Dropping the Freedom 40 conditions; promising custom matching filters ([[matching-and-call]]).
- Em dashes in new copy (house rule for this project; quotes above keep the source's punctuation).
