# Visual identity

Two looks exist. The core site and the Delegation Audit are dark with green. The older funnels (HIRE book, Delegation Mastermind) are blue and white. For new work aimed at founders, match paretotalent.com. See [[brand-voice]] for tone.

## Palette (paretotalent.com)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #050A0E |
| Surfaces | #0B1119, #0F1923 |
| Primary green | #10B981 |
| Light green | #34D399 |
| Bright green | #6EE7B7 |
| Amber accent ("ember") | #E0973F (also #F2B968) |
| Text primary | #F0F4F8 |
| Text secondary | #94A3B8 |
| Text muted | #64748B |

The Delegation Audit uses a slightly different green set (#33E39E, #5EF2BB, #0F9D63) on #080B0A ([[existing-lead-magnets]]).

## Logo
Blue wordmark, "Pareto" over "talent", blocky hand-cut lettering, color #468FFE on transparent: https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/KjTTNn7q7Rl3zed7tzFS/media/65d4092400a2ec10ef5c1bef.png. A white variant is used inside the Delegation Audit. The blue appears in the logo and in the legacy funnels (HIRE book, Delegation Mastermind: #188BF6, #1E61D6, navy #000321).

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Headings: Hanken Grotesk, weight 800, letter-spacing -0.04em, line-height about 1.02 (the site also uses Plus Jakarta Sans 800 for headings and buttons).
- Body: DM Sans 400 to 600.
- Small labels and mono accents: Space Grotesk (home); IBM Plex Mono (Delegation Audit).

## Buttons
- Primary: vertical green gradient #34D399 to #10B981, dark text #050A0E, weight 800, radius 12px.
- Home hero CTA: flat #50D3A1, dark text #04120C, weight 800, radius 14px.
- Ghost: transparent, 2px #10B981 border, green text.
- Arrow glyph in copy: "Get Matched →". Never "Submit".

## Cards and surfaces
Glass cards, 20 to 24px radius, 1px border at white 6 percent, subtle blur, fine grain and soft green glows.

## Imagery
3D "clay-like" miniature dioramas: Pixar-style warm characters with felt or knit textures on round stepped stone platforms, monstera and succulent plants, wood furniture, deep teal and forest green, a top spotlight, neon-green light trails showing AI and automation. Examples: https://paretotalent.com/img/hero-diorama.jpg, /img/offer-core.jpg, /img/offer-mastermind.jpg. Real photos and video thumbnails are used on the [[wall-of-love]] and referral pages. Mood: dark cinematic and warm human, "calm after chaos".

## Bootcamp rule
One visual identity across ads, pages, emails and the lead magnet (Day 10 deck, slides 39 and 42). See [[final-project-guidelines]] and [[bootcamp-frameworks]].
