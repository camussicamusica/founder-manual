## Method rules used
- Every claim traced to a source in the file's frontmatter or inline.
- Numbers carry VERIFIED-SITE, VERIFIED-TRANSCRIPT, UNVERIFIED or CONFLICT; competitor post-opt-in steps carry OBSERVED or INFERRED.
- Nothing submitted, downloaded or entered on any competitor or Pareto form.
- Transcript names that the speech-to-text garbled were normalised (for example "Casim" to Kasim, "Presta Talent" to Pareto Talent) and noted where relevant.
