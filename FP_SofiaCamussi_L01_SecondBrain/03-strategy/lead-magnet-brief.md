# Lead magnet brief

## Name
**Get Your Business Out of Your Head in 10 Minutes: The Founder Manual Builder.** Short name: "The Founder Manual Builder".

## MAGIC breakdown ([[bootcamp-frameworks]], section 1)
| Letter | Meaning | Here |
|---|---|---|
| M Magnet | the hook | get your business out of your head |
| A Avatar | who it is for | the founder |
| G Goal | the result | a one-page operating manual someone else can run your week from |
| I Interval | the time frame | 10 minutes |
| C Container | what it is | Builder (a mini web app) |

## Format and the one problem it solves
A static mini web app that runs in the browser with no login. Problem: "everything runs from memory" plus "I don't have time to train someone right now" (founder words in [[personas]] P1 and P3). In 10 minutes the founder turns the rules in their head into a one-page manual a Right Hand, any hire, or their AI can work from on day one.

## Why this and not a quiz or a task list
Pareto already owns the Delegation Audit and the HIRE book ([[existing-lead-magnets]]). Every competitor answers "what can I hand off?" ([[market-patterns]]). Nobody answers "how do I hand it off without spending weeks training?", the objection that stops a founder from booking.

## Value Equation ([[bootcamp-frameworks]], section 2)
| Lever | Move |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome (up) | a week that runs without me |
| Likelihood of achievement (up) | built on what Pareto's 100+ founders and 250+ Right Hands need on day one (both VERIFIED, see [[proof-bank]]) |
| Time delay (down) | 10 minutes, the result is on screen instantly |
| Effort and sacrifice (down) | tap chips instead of writing from scratch; no signup needed to use it |

## What is inside (12 prompts, mostly chips plus short text)
1 me and the business in one line · 2 my week's rhythm · 3 how to reach me by urgency · 4 decisions you can make without me (including a spend limit) · 5 decisions that are always mine · 6 protect at all costs · 7 how to lose my trust · 8 how I want updates · 9 what I still do myself (task checklist with hours a week) · 10 tools and where things live · 11 my definition of done · 12 three things I want off my plate in week 1.

## Outputs
1. A one-page Founder Manual, printable or saved as PDF.
2. A "Copy as AI context" Markdown block that works as custom instructions or a CLAUDE.md.
3. A Week-1 Handoff List: top 3 tasks to hand off first and hours a week recovered, computed live from prompt 9. No hard-coded number.

## Who counts as qualified (exact rule)
Form fields: first name, last name, email, company or website, role, annual revenue, team size, hours a week lost to admin, when they want help.
- Role: Founder, owner or CEO / Executive or manager (not the owner) / Assistant or looking for work / Other.
- Revenue: Under $100K / $100K to $500K / $500K to $1M / $1M to $5M / $5M+.
- Team: Just me / 2 to 5 / 6 to 15 / 16 to 50 / 50+.
- Admin hours: Under 5 / 5 to 10 / 10 to 20 / 20+.
- Timing: As soon as possible / In the next 90 days / Just exploring.

**QUALIFIED = role is "Founder, owner or CEO" AND revenue is $500K or more.** Everything else is unqualified. Extra tag `priority::hot` when qualified AND timing is ASAP or 90 days AND admin hours are 10 or more.
Why: the ICP is business owners at about $500K+ ([[icp]]); the program starts at $3,600 a month plus $3,000 placement ([[pareto-talent-identity]]); a role question keeps job seekers out of the sales calendar, which competitors do too ([[market-patterns]]). The form shape follows Pareto's own HIRE qualifier ([[existing-lead-magnets]]) and Day 6 ([[bootcamp-frameworks]], section 6).

## Routing
Qualified goes to a booking page with a calendar embedded, the manual link and what happens on the call. Unqualified goes to a thank-you page that confirms and delivers the resource plus two free next steps, no call offered.

## Next-step offer (for qualified founders)
"Bring your Founder Manual to a 20-minute Matching Call. We map what to hand off first, and if we're a fit you meet 3 hand-picked Right Hands within 24 hours. You owe nothing unless you find your person."
The "20-minute" length is our proposal; Pareto's pages do not state a call length ([[matching-and-call]]). Proof allowed next to the offer: 100+ founders, 250+ Right Hands, top 1% of applicants, 40+ hours of AI training before placement, three guarantees with Freedom 40's conditions ([[proof-bank]]).

## Closing CTA inside the app
"You wrote the manual. Pareto has the Right Hand who can run it." Button goes to the landing page form anchor. Button copy never "Submit".
