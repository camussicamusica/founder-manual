# Pareto's ICP

## In one paragraph
A US-based founder or owner-operator of a service, agency, coaching or expert business doing roughly $500K to $10M a year with 2 to 50 people, who is still the approval, the fix-it and the final word. They work nights and weekends, run the company from memory and have often been burned by a cheap assistant before. They do not need more tasks done; they need a proactive operator who closes loops without being chased.
