# Five founder personas by cause

A persona is defined by the cause that brings the founder to the service, not by demographics (Day 8). Same offer, different persona, different angle, different ad. "Client words" are verbatim from the source named. Pains are drawn from the same sources. Funnel stage says where the ad for that persona sits ([[bootcamp-frameworks]], section 5). Fit with the ICP: [[icp]].

## P1. The Memory Runner (no systems)
- Cause: everything is in the founder's head, so everything routes through them.
- Client words: "Everything still routes through me. I'm the approval, the fix-it, the final word." (Delegation Audit answer, [[existing-lead-magnets]]); "Everything Runs From Memory: No systems, no SOPs. Every process depends on you being present. Fragile." (home, [[home-page]]); "I'm the bottleneck in my own company" (Day 8 client-words example, D8 L1401-1417).
- Pains: fragile processes, nothing documented, cannot step away.
- Angle: get it out of your head once; stop being the speed limit.
- Funnel stage: TOFU.

## P2. The Once-Burned (a past assistant failed)
- Cause: a previous VA or EA created more work, so trust is gone.
- Client words: "I've tried a VA before and it created more work." (home FAQ); "I've had three different executive assistants before, and I thought it was me, right? I was burning through them." (Eric Ritter, [[wall-of-love]]); "I've been through a few assistants already. I'm not sure the next one will be any different." (Delegation Audit answer).
- Pains: management overhead, no vetting, no accountability ("Burned by a VA Before", home).
- Angle: it was not you and it was not them; nobody had the manual.
- Funnel stage: MOFU.

## P3. The No-Time-To-Train (too busy to onboard)
- Cause: onboarding someone feels like another job.
- Client words: "I don't have time to train someone right now." (home FAQ); "It's faster if I just do it myself. I don't even know what I'd hand off." (Delegation Audit answer); Delegation Audit option "No time to train someone".
- Pains: no time to explain, unsure what to delegate, repeating instructions.
- Angle: 10 minutes replaces weeks of training.
- Funnel stage: MOFU.

## P4. The Stage-Bound Expert (speaker, coach, consultant; the brand is them)
- Cause: the founder is the product, so a helper must sound like them and use their judgment.
- Client words: "Clients pay for time with me." (Delegation Audit archetype, "The Stage-Bound Founder"); Day 8 board persona 2: speaker, coach or consultant, pain "sound like me" and a previous VA that sent AI fluff (paraphrase from the frameworks sheet, D8 L1585-1657, see [[bootcamp-frameworks]]; the exact spoken words were not captured).
- Pains: personality match, voice, quality of output.
- Angle: teach someone your voice and judgment, on one page.
- Funnel stage: MOFU.

## P5. The Ready Buyer (already wants help, comparing options)
- Cause: has decided to hire and is choosing how.
- Client words: "I wish I'd started 12–18 months earlier." (Freedom 40 page, https://pic-perfect-render-69.lovable.app/; official status of that page UNVERIFIED). The home page says clients' "only regret is the 12 to 18 months they spent in the bottleneck" (VERIFIED-SITE, [[home-page]]).
- Pains: cost of waiting, fear of a bad match, wants options and a guarantee.
- Angle: bring your manual to a Matching Call; 3 hand-picked matches within 24 hours ([[matching-and-call]]).
- Funnel stage: BOFU.

## Use
Five ads equals P1 to P5 with five different messages, each with hook, offer, qualifier, proof, mechanism, call to action. The lead magnet that serves all five is in [[lead-magnet-brief]]. Proof to attach to each ad: [[proof-bank]].
